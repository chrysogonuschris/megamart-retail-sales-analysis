# MegaMart Retail Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the key factors influencing the overall business performance of the MegaMart retail store, with a focus on **sales, profit, customers, products and discounts**.

The analysis covers product and category performance, sales trends, store and regional performance, customer demographics, average order value (AOV), customer lifetime value (CLV), and the effect of discounts on revenue.

> **Project documentation:** The completed analytical report is provided in the `documentation` folder. The original working Excel/Power BI files are not included in this repository.

## Business Objectives

- Identify which categories and products drive sales and profit the most.
- Identify sales trends over time by month, quarter and year.
- Identify which store and region perform best.
- Identify how customer demographics influence sales.
- Identify average order value (AOV) and customer lifetime value (CLV).
- Identify how discounts affect revenue.

## Data Overview

The report used four tables:

- Sales transactions
- Customer information
- Product information
- Store information

**Source:** Kaggle datasets.

Derived analytical fields included customer age, customer age band, customer lifespan and discount band.

## Key Findings

### Product & Category Performance

MegaMart's portfolio contains Electronics, Fashion and Groceries. **Fashion** was the strongest-performing category, generating **$6.23M in sales, $1.66M in profit and 7,015 units sold**.

Within Fashion, **Accessories** led the product segment with **$1.92M sales, $431.99K profit and 1,612 units sold**.

### Sales Trends

The transaction period covered **September 2023 to September 2025**.

The report found that sales increased from 2023 to 2024 and then experienced a slight decline in 2025. The fourth quarter contributed the most to 2024 performance, while sales consistently declined during the second and third quarters. Sales peaked around **November–December**, with **February** recording the lowest sales.

### Store & Regional Performance

The **East region** generated **$5.60M sales, $1.50M profit and 5,834 units sold**.

The strongest individual branch was **MegaMart New Michele** in the West, with **$2.92M sales, $804.01K gross profit, 27.53% profit margin and 3,036 units sold**.

### Customer Demographics

Customers in their forties generated **$1.23M sales and $322.44K gross profit**, with 444 units purchased.

Male customers generated **$8.07M sales and $2.17M gross profit**, with 2,810 units purchased.

Among the cities analysed, **Lake Dawn** recorded the highest sales at **$117.09K**, with **$28.43K gross profit** from 32 units sold.

### AOV & CLV

The reported **average order value was approximately $2.86K**.

The report identifies average order value, purchase frequency and customer lifespan as important factors influencing customer lifetime value. **Brittany Green** recorded the highest CLV at approximately **$143.367K**, with an eight-year customer lifespan identified as the main factor behind the result.

### Discount Analysis

| Discount band | Sales | Gross profit | Transactions | Units | Reported impact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–10% | $7.01M | $1.88M | 2,433 | 7,261 | 7.46% |
| No discount | $3.85M | $1.24M | 1,243 | 3,732 | — |
| 11–20% | $3.44M | $707.79K | 1,324 | 3,956 | 15% |

The report found that the **1–10% discount band generated the highest sales and gross profit** among the analysed discount groups, while the 11–20% band produced lower sales and profitability with a higher reported impact on revenue.

## Recommendations

1. Increase focus and investment in **Groceries**, which recorded the highest profit margin at **30.44%**, including expanding the product range and maintaining stock availability.
2. Ensure consistent stock availability during the **end-of-year period**, when recurring seasonal demand was identified.
3. Investigate the **South region**, particularly MegaMart Jimenezborough, to understand why it recorded higher unit sales but lower overall performance than MegaMart New Michele.
4. Introduce **VIP loyalty programmes** for high-value customers and customers who contribute significantly to sales and profitability over a specified period.
